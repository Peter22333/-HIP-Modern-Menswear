Our Story

THE STORY BEHIND HIP.

HIP exists for a simple reason: modern men needed suits that matched the pace and ambition of their lives. This is how we build them.

Mission

TAILORING FOR TODAY'S MAN.

HIP was founded to close the gap between traditional tailoring and modern life. We design suits with contemporary proportions, breathable construction and clean detailing — built for men who move between the boardroom, the wedding and everything after.

Values

CONFIDENCE, QUALITY, SPEED.

Every HIP suit is judged against three standards: does it look sharp under any light, is it built to last a season of hard wear, and can we get it to you without the months-long wait traditional tailoring demands. If a design doesn't clear all three, it doesn't ship.

Approach to Menswear

MODERN DESIGN, HONEST CONSTRUCTION.

We keep silhouettes lean and current, fabrics practical for real climates, and finishing details — buttons, linings, stitching — held to a professional standard. Nothing about a HIP suit is decorative for its own sake.

Quality & Turnaround

SHARP, ON YOUR SCHEDULE.

We've streamlined production so you're not waiting months for a suit you need this quarter. Quick turnaround, without cutting corners on quality — that's the HIP standard.
